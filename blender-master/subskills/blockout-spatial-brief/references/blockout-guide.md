# Blockout guide

## Scale cheatsheet (metres, typical)

| Thing | Size |
|---|---|
| adult standing | 1.6-1.85 tall, eye 1.5-1.7 |
| door | 0.8-0.9 x 2.0-2.1 |
| ceiling (home) | 2.4-2.7 |
| table / desk top | 0.72-0.76 high |
| chair seat | 0.43-0.47 high |
| bed (double) | 1.4 x 2.0, mattress top 0.5-0.6 |
| kitchen counter | 0.9 high, 0.6 deep |
| window sill | 0.8-1.0 high |
| car | 4.2-4.8 x 1.8 x 1.45 |
| street lane | 3.0-3.7 wide |
| perfume bottle | 0.05-0.1 wide, 0.1-0.15 tall |
| phone | 0.072 x 0.008 x 0.15 |
| wooden crate | 0.4-0.8 per side |

## Lens choice

| Subject | Lens (full-frame equivalent) | Camera height |
|---|---|---|
| interior, whole room | 18-28 mm | 1.2-1.7 m, keep verticals straight (camera pitch near 90 deg) |
| environment, street | 24-35 mm | eye height or drone-high |
| prop, hero object | 35-60 mm | slightly above the top or slightly below for heroics |
| product packshot | 70-100 mm | at or just below the product's middle |
| portrait / character bust | 70-135 mm | eye level |
| technical views | orthographic | front / side / top |

Wide lenses exaggerate depth and make spaces feel larger; long lenses flatten and flatter.

## Composition checks for the hero preview
- The subject fills roughly 40-80 % of the short frame side unless the brief says otherwise.
- Strong vertical lines stay vertical (use a level camera plus lens shift instead of tilting).
- Leave breathing room on the side the subject faces or moves toward.
- Horizon not cutting through a subject's head or the exact middle of the frame.
- Background shapes do not touch the subject's silhouette edge (tangents read as one blob).

## Proxy notes that help later parts
Good: "glass body, 2 mm walls, flat front for label", "crate lid must lift off: separate object".
Weak: "bottle", "box". The note is the contract the modelling part is checked against.

## Review layer usage
1. Render the hero preview, or look through the camera.
2. Draw red around what is wrong and green where something should be added, each with a note
   (`review_mark(points, kind, note)`).
3. The next part lists each note with "done" or a reason.
