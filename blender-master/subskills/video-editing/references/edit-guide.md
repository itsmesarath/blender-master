# Edit guide

## Pacing
- Product ads: hook in the first 1-2 s, one idea per shot, 1.5-3 s per shot, end card 2-3 s.
- Turntables: one full slow turn (6-10 s), no cuts.
- Story shorts: cut on action; hold reaction shots a beat longer than feels necessary.
- Transitions: straight cuts by default; crossfades (6-12 frames at 24 fps) for time or mood
  shifts; fades to black only at the end or at big breaks.

## Delivery specs (common)
| Target | Size | fps | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| landscape HD | 1920x1080 | 24/25/30 | H.264 high quality, AAC 192 kbps |
| vertical short | 1080x1920 | 30 | keep text out of the bottom 20% and the right edge |
| square | 1080x1080 | 30 | |
| 4K master | 3840x2160 | as rendered | archive also the frame sequence |

## Titles
- Safe area: about 5% inset for action, 10% for titles.
- Contrast: shadow or a dark card behind light text; avoid thin fonts at small sizes.
- On-screen time: roughly 0.3 s per word, at least 1.5 s.

## Audio
- Music under dialogue around -18 to -24 dB below speech; overall peaks below -1 dBFS.
- Fade music in 0.3-1 s and out 1-3 s; never cut sound mid-note at the end.
- Generated tones are placeholders for timing; replace with licensed music for delivery.

## Workflow
- Keep the edit in its own scene or file; frames come in as sequences.
- Re-encode from frames for each delivery size instead of scaling an encode.
