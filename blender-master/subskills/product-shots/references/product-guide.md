# Product guide

## Lighting by material
| Material | Light |
|---|---|
| glossy plastic, lacquer | large soft top + two long strips at the sides for edge lines |
| glass, liquids | backlight through the product (strip or panel behind), dark flags at the sides for black edge lines |
| metal (polished) | big gradients: large panels, reflectors (white cards); the environment is the product |
| brushed metal | strip lights along the brushing direction |
| matte, fabric | soft key at 45 deg, low fill, texture-revealing grazing light |
| cosmetics, jewellery | small hard accents for sparkle plus a soft base light |

## Shot list (e-commerce)
1. Hero 3/4, camera slightly below the product top, 85-100 mm.
2. Front (label square to camera).
3. Side / back (ingredients, ports).
4. Top-down flat lay for groups.
5. Detail crops: texture, cap, logo, with shallow depth of field.
6. Scale shot (in hand or next to a known object).

## Ad timing (24-30 fps)
| Beat | Duration |
|---|---|
| establishing / reveal | 1-2 s |
| hero move (turn, lift) | 2-4 s |
| feature beats | 1-2 s each |
| logo / end card hold | 2-3 s |
Social loops: 6-10 s, seamless, product centred (safe for square and vertical crops).

## Framing
- Action-safe 90%, title-safe 80% of the frame (`safe_area` with 0.9 / 0.8).
- Leave space on one side for copy text in banners.
- For vertical (9:16) and square deliveries, plan a centred composition or separate cameras.

## Backdrops
- Seamless sweep (no horizon line), colour graded toward the brand palette.
- Contact shadow under the product grounds it; avoid floating products unless stylized.
